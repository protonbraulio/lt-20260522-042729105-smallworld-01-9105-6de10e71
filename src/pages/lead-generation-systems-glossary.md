## Definition

Lead generation systems refer to the processes, tools, and strategies used to identify, attract, and convert potential customers into leads for a business. In the context of sales teams qualifying higher-intent leads in Melbourne, a robust lead generation system ensures that marketing and sales efforts are aligned, and that leads are passed on efficiently and effectively.

## Example

Consider a Melbourne-based software company that implements a lead generation system using marketing automation tools.

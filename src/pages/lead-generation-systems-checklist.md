## Readiness criteria

Before diving into the lead generation systems implementation, ensure the following criteria are met to set your project up for success in Melbourne.

First, identify and confirm the **owner** of the lead generation systems process. This person will be responsible for driving the project forward and ensuring its success.

Next, gather and verify the **required inputs**. This includes data sources, tools, and any other resources necessary for the implementation. Ensure all inputs are accessible and functional.

Clearly define the **expected outcome** of the lead generation systems implementation. This could include increased lead quality, reduced lead time, or improved conversion rates.

Establish **decision criteria** to evaluate the success of the lead generation systems implementation. This could be a specific metric, such as a reduction in lead time, or a qualitative assessment of lead quality.

## Implementation steps

Implementing lead generation systems in Melbourne involves several key steps. Follow these concrete examples and decision criteria to ensure a successful rollout.

1. **Assess your current lead generation processes**: Begin by evaluating your existing lead generation methods. Identify strengths and weaknesses to inform your new system.

2. **Design the new lead generation system**: Based on your assessment, design a new system that addresses the identified gaps and improves upon existing processes. Consider the unique needs of your sales teams qualifying higher-intent leads.

3. **Integrate new tools and technologies**: Implement any new tools or technologies required for the new lead generation system. Ensure they are compatible with your existing tech stack and that your team is trained on their use.

4. **Test the new system**: Conduct thorough testing of the new lead generation system to identify and address any issues before full-scale implementation. This could include A/B testing or piloting the system with a small group of users.

5. **Roll out the new system**: Once testing is complete and any issues have been addressed, roll out the new lead generation system to your team. Ensure all users are trained on the new process and have access to any necessary resources.

## Validation checks

After implementing the lead generation systems, it's crucial to validate its effectiveness. Use the following checks and metrics to ensure the system is working as intended in Melbourne.

1. **Lead quality**: Measure the quality of leads generated by the new system. This could include metrics such as lead-to-opportunity conversion rate, lead-to-close conversion rate, or lead scoring.

2. **Lead time**: Track the time it takes for leads to progress through the sales pipeline. A reduction in lead time indicates an improvement in lead generation efficiency.

3. **Lead volume**: Monitor the volume of leads generated by the new system. While increased volume is not always indicative of improved quality, it can be a useful metric to track alongside lead quality metrics.

4. **User feedback**: Gather feedback from your sales teams qualifying higher-intent leads on the new lead generation system. Their insights can provide valuable context and help identify areas for improvement.

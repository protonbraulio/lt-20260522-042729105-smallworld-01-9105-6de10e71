## Detailed explanation

The lead generation systems workflow in Melbourne involves several key steps to ensure clear handoffs and practical checks. First, identify the owner of the lead generation system, who will be responsible for its maintenance and updates.

Next, determine the required inputs for the system. These could include lead sources, lead scoring criteria, and lead assignment rules. Ensure that these inputs are clearly defined and consistently applied.

The expected outcome of the lead generation system should be clearly communicated to all stakeholders. This could include the number of qualified leads generated, the lead conversion rate, or the return on investment (ROI) for the system.

Decision criteria should be established to evaluate the performance of the lead generation system. These criteria could include lead quality, lead velocity, and lead-to-customer conversion rate. Regularly review and update these criteria to ensure they remain relevant and effective.

To measure the success of the lead generation system, establish metrics that align with the expected outcome and decision criteria. These metrics could include the number of leads generated, the lead conversion rate, or the cost per acquisition (CPA). Regularly track and analyze these metrics to identify trends and areas for improvement.
